Nintendo Console

Information

Nintendo is a Japanese multinational entertainment company founded in 1889, known for its video game consoles and franchises. The company has a history of creating popular consoles and characters, including the Nintendo Entertainment System (NES), Game Boy, Super Nintendo Entertainment System (SNES), Wii, Nintendo 3DS, Nintendo Switch, and many characters like Mario, Luigi, and Link. Nintendo's mission is to deliver unique entertainment experiences and put smiles on faces worldwide
