PlayStation Console

Information

PlayStation, a video game console and entertainment system manufactured by Sony, offers various models including the PS5 (standard and Digital Edition), PS5 Slim, and PS5 Pro, with prices ranging from $399 to $699. PlayStation Plus, a subscription service, offers tiered access to online multiplayer, exclusive games, and more, with monthly or annual options available.

Price

The standard PS5 offers both physical and digital game access with a storage capacity of 825 GB and a price of $399.99 for the Digital Edition and $449.99 for the standard model. PS5 Slim: The PS5 Slim retains the same features as the standard PS5 but with a slimmer design, according to Wired. It also has a removable disc drive and comes in both Digital Edition and standard models. The digital edition costs $449.99 while the standard model costs $499.99.

Latest News

Sony unveiled its first official upscaler, PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR), alongside the PlayStation 5 Pro. While PSSR can upscale smaller resolutions to 4K, newer graphics technologies like frame generation can introduce additional latency. Consequently, while you receive more frames, your games may not feel as responsive. To address this issue, GPU manufacturers AMD and Nvidia have introduced Radeon Anti-Lag and Nvidia Reflex, respectively. Now, it seems Sony is developing its own solution.
