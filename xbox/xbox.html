Information

Xbox is a video gaming brand that consists of four main home video game console lines, as well as applications (games), the streaming service Xbox Cloud Gaming, and online services such as the Xbox network and Xbox Game Pass. The brand is produced by Microsoft Gaming, a division of Microsoft. The brand was introduced in 2001 with the original Xbox console and has evolved into a multi-platform gaming ecosystem.

Price

The Xbox Series S has a starter bundle price of $299.99 and a 1TB model at $349.99. The Xbox Series X is available for $36.99 per month with Xbox All Access. A certified refurbished Xbox Series X is also available for $429.99.

Latest News

Xbox (2001): The original console, the first American-made console after the Atari Jaguar. Xbox 360 (2005): The second generation console, known for its popularity and large installed base. Xbox One (2013): Replaced the Xbox 360 and aimed to improve upon the previous console's issues. Xbox One S (2016): A revised version of the Xbox One with a smaller form factor. Xbox One X (2017): A high-end model of the Xbox One with upgraded hardware. Xbox Series X & Series S (2020): The latest generation of consoles, featuring faster loading times and higher resolution graphics.
